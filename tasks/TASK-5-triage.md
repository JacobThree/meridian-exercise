Open `comms/inbox.md`. Five messages, one technical person, a day with other things in it.

## Write up, in `answers/task5.md`

1. **Order the five messages** in the order you would deal with them, one line of reasoning each.

2. **Write two replies**, exactly as you'd send them: the CEO (message 1) and the vendor (message 4).
